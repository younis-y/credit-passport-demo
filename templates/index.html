Read the limits

Arithmetic is not authenticity.

A fabricated statement that adds up can pass. These checks do not verify a bank, account holder, employer or source of funds. Recurring credits are an estimate of income; rent can be inferred from payment patterns. The first balance has no previous row to check.

Payment timing is compared with the usual day in the data, not a contractual due date. This prototype is not a credit score, affordability assessment or lending decision.
